AI for Security: Agents I've Worked On

A personal project: eight AI agents that automate core security-operations and risk work, each producing evidence-backed, sourced output.

AI Security Agents

View on GitHub →
team-manager

Routes a security request to the right agents or human teams in a safe order, then merges their reports into one summary where every conclusion names its source.

vm-triage

Researches one CVE from public sources (KEV, EPSS, CVSS, exploitation, IOCs, detections, fixes) and produces a cited 26-section report.

vulnerability-hunt

Takes vm-triage's output plus your inventory and decides, asset by asset, whether you're affected and exposed and how urgently to act, with fix-verification steps and ticket drafts.

soc-analyst

Two jobs: triages one alert and recommends one of seven dispositions with evidence, a timeline and ATT&CK mapping; or hunts for threats no alert caught and reports Found / Not found / Inconclusive with data coverage.

cloud-security-analyst

Finds real attack paths in cloud IaC, IAM and CSPM data, and triages cloud alerts by correlating them with audit logs.

threat-modeler

Builds an evidence-based threat model from code and design documents: a data-flow diagram reviewed by a human, STRIDE threats, validation of existing controls and prioritised risks.

threat-intel-analyst

Profiles a threat actor, campaign or indicator set from public sources: ATT&CK techniques, IOCs, attribution with competing hypotheses, published detection rules and STIX-style output.

third-party-risk-analyst (in progress)

Assesses a vendor across its whole life: tiers it by inherent risk × criticality, checks its answers against evidence (SOC 2, certificates, contract), finds gaps and prepares a decision pack for a named approver.
